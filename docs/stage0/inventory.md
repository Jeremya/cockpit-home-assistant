## Real-device proof scope — 2026-10-15

**Kasa:** the actual HA backend and visibility/room selectors find an available switch, visible by default, in its assigned area, with fresh entity/device/area registries. Its configuration LED is hidden by default. A read-only state check during the CEO review succeeds. This integration exposed and fixed normalization of HA `area_id` into the app's internal area `id` in `17827d0`; regressions cover initial grouping, failed-refresh retention and authoritative area removal.

**Petlibro:** after the share was accepted and the integration reloaded, HA discovered the feeder. Its 42 registered entities include domains outside Maurice's supported read-only set; **23 readings appear in the actual backend snapshot and are visible by default**. Account/pet registry entries are not additional physical feeders. Data includes last/next distribution, quantities, connection, food status and battery. These are manufacturer/HA reports, not proof the cat ate. Initial integration values required a poll before useful telemetry appeared; do not classify every zero as a fault. Battery `low` needs interpretation against the actual power/battery setup.

Neither proof sent device service calls, dispensed food or modified a schedule. Detailed identities, network addresses and payloads remain in ignored `.local/` evidence. The household locations have been assigned in HA; no device is placed on the drawing yet (P03/P04).

## Remaining acceptance and hosting

- Verify both devices' presentation on the installed phone; add U03 summaries and U04 attention rules before treating the overview as a complete household assessment.
- Keep HA's built-in demo entities separate from the two proven real devices and from the app's offline demo.
- Controls require C01–C04 and the corresponding capability-gated UI. Real actions need their own authorized physical acceptance; a working Kasa switch does not certify locks, fans, climate or feeder control.
- Camera and BLE paths remain open. Do not infer still-image availability from a camera entity or vent support from a brand.

The dedicated HA box is H02, still future work. Changing origin creates a new local profile. Preserve preferences through an explicit migration and never automatically reuse a token across origins. A standalone APK removes Metro dependency, not dependency on the HA host.
